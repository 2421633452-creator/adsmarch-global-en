04AI + Expert

AI provides the efficiency and the insight,
experts provide the judgment and the execution.

It is easy to call AI a service. It is hard to say exactly where it takes part. Below are the four stages where it genuinely contributes, and the four things it cannot replace.

AI

Intelligence & Efficiency
InsightsInsight
Retrieving and consolidating market and competitor material, surfacing themes and keywords, and compressing days of manual reading into something you can actually make a call on.
StrategyStrategy
Based on your growth goal and requirements, we combine capabilities into candidate options and explain the reasoning. This is not a decision made for you; it narrows the options to a set worth discussing.
ExecutionExecution
Batch creative production, multi-market and multi-size adaptation, and localised first drafts of copy - taking repetitive work off people's plates.
OptimisationOptimization
Bringing media, content and store data into one place, flagging anomalies and trends, and feeding both weekly reviews and monthly business reviews.

Expert

Judgment & Strategy
JudgmentJudgment
Which market to enter first, which route is not worth taking, when to stop. These depend on an understanding of the industry and the competitive landscape that no model can supply.
Trade-offsTrade-offs
How the budget is split, which creative direction to take, which tier of influencers to use - the same goal can be approached several ways, and choosing one requires someone to be accountable.
ProgressExecution
Day-to-day progress and commercial conversations with media platforms, influencers, creators and local partners. This part is person to person.
AccountableAccountability
When results go wrong, a person works out what broke and how to fix it, rather than waiting for an algorithm to converge on its own.

AI + Expert =Measurable, scalable global growthMeasurable & Scalable Growth

At AdsMarch, AI is not a service you can buy on its own. It sits inside every stage above. You can see exactly where it takes part and where it stops. Where a decision needs an accountable owner, we do not hand it to an algorithm.

This is not a slogan

The recommendations inside the Growth Plan Builder are the smallest working version of that division of labour. Once you set your goal and requirements, the system applies rules to flag which capabilities are relevant and explains why. A consultant then turns that structure into an executable plan. AI structures the opportunity. Experts turn it into strategy.
