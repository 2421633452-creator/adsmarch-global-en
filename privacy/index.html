For a website aimed at overseas clients, a privacy policy is a compliance necessity. This document carries legal liability and must be confirmed by AdsMarch legal counsel or an external lawyer before it goes live.

The structure confirmed for this page

CONTENT PENDING · Structure first, so material can be collected block by block

01

Why it is required

GDPR applies to EU visitors and CCPA to California visitors. A form without a privacy policy directly reduces submission rates from overseas clients.

  • GDPR: EU visitors have the right to know how their data is used and to request deletion and export
  • CCPA: California visitors have the right to request that their personal information is not sold
  • If analytics tools or advertising pixels are used, a cookie consent mechanism is also required
02

What this page needs to cover

to be completed once legal counsel confirms it.

  • What information is collected (form fields, cookies, analytics data)
  • How information is used and how long it is kept
  • Whether data is shared with third parties, and with whom
  • Visitors' rights and how to exercise them (access, correction, deletion, export)
  • How cross-border data transfers are handled
  • Contact details and data protection officer
03

Relationship to this prototype

The prototype's form is not connected to a backend; nothing is uploaded or retained.

  • After submission the form only generates a local brief for the visitor to download
  • No input is retained after the page is closed
  • This page and the consent checkbox must go live at the same time as the backend
