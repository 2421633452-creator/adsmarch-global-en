E-COMMERCE GROWTH
From Market Entry to Global Commerce.

From choosing markets and localising, to building the site and conversion infrastructure, then acquisition, conversion and repeat purchase, and finally multi-market scale. E-commerce growth is a chain you can break into stages, each with its own deliverables and measurement basis.

How to use this page:The seven stages form one complete commercial chain, and the order itself is the recommended sequence. Find the part you are stuck on and read from there. If you want to build out the capability list first, you can start fromCapability listhere.

01

Market

Decide where to go first

“Not sure whether to start with the US or Europe, and no confidence about which channel to start with either.”

Before spending, get a clear view of the market, category, competitors and target audience, then decide the pace of entry.

Used at this stage

  • Market and competitor analysis to define the entry category and price band
  • Target audience and purchase scenario insights
  • Market entry priorities and pacing recommendations
  • Budget allocation and stage goal definition

Localize

Make a global brand look local

“The creative is obviously translated.”

Localisation is not translation. It is switching to the way local consumers already express themselves.

Used at this stage

  • Rewriting localised copy, visuals and modes of expression
  • Multilingual creative and landing page adaptation
  • Local social content cadence and account operations
  • Cultural difference and compliance risk guidance

Build

Build the place where the sale happens

“The site loads, but the conversion path is very long.”

Site, products, tracking and conversion infrastructure - first make it capable of catching the traffic, then talk about scaling.

Used at this stage

  • DTC site build and conversion path design
  • Product page, landing page and checkout flow configuration
  • Tracking, attribution and data dashboard build
  • Conversion infrastructure audit and prioritised improvement recommendations

Related capabilities

Commerce & RevenueData & Growth Optimization
04

Acquire

Turn demand into traffic

“The budget gets spent, but growth does not move.”

Acquiring new customers through mainstream channels and the influencer ecosystem while holding unit acquisition cost down.

Used at this stage

  • Google / Meta / TikTok multi-channel media buying and budget allocation
  • Influencer and creator collaborations covering content seeding and sales-driving scenarios
  • Creative testing and iteration cadence
  • Coordinated optimisation of channel mix, audience structure and landing pages

Related capabilities

Media & User AcquisitionInfluencer & SocialCreative & Content
05

Convert

Turn traffic into orders

“Plenty of add-to-carts, but they are always lost at the final step.”

Breaking the conversion funnel into segments to find the one that is actually stuck, rather than increasing the budget across the board.

Used at this stage

  • Conversion rate diagnosis using heatmaps and A/B testing
  • Product page and landing page optimisation
  • Promotion, bundle and seasonal campaign design
  • Checkout flow and trust element optimisation

Related capabilities

Commerce & RevenueData & Growth OptimizationCreative & Content
06

Retain

Turn customers into repeat buyers

“Repeat purchase depends entirely on discounts; stop the promotion and it drops.”

Using email and a membership programme to turn a one-off sale into a long-term relationship you can manage.

Used at this stage

  • Abandoned cart recovery and automated email flow deployment
  • User segmentation and personalised outreach
  • On-site repeat purchase operations and exclusive offers for returning customers
  • Repeat purchase rate and LTV tracking

Related capabilities

Commerce & RevenueData & Growth Optimization
07

Scale

Run several markets at once

“The second market means building it all over again.”

One system, many markets - replicating what works rather than rebuilding it in the next market.

Used at this stage

  • Multi-market expansion path and prioritisation
  • Producing localised creative and campaign assets at scale
  • Cross-market budget and channel reallocation
  • Unified regional data definitions and coordinated reviews

SHARED FOUNDATION

AI + EXPERT

AI and experts do not do the same job. One narrows possibilities into candidates; the other decides which candidate is worth doing.

Intelligence · Automation · Optimization

AI

Converging data into candidates: which keywords are worth bidding on, which creative is losing volume, which market has the lower marginal cost. It improves speed and coverage - it does not decide your direction for you.

Strategy · Judgment · Creativity · Execution

EXPERT

Judging which candidate is worth doing, how to split the budget, when to stop. That determines direction, and it is the part we are accountable for.
