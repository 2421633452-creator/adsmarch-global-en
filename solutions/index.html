APP GROWTH

From first install to sustainable growth

“Volume comes in but does not stay, and the money feels poured away.”

App growth is a user lifecycle chain: be found, be downloaded, then activated, retained and monetised, and finally replicated into more markets. Each of the six stages has its own measurement basis and maps to capability modules in active use.

This path covers

  • Visibility optimisation across the store page and search environment, so the app is found first
  • Multi-channel acquisition and account structure optimisation: building volume, and pulling quality back up
  • Breaking down the activation funnel and segmenting retention: turning installs into active and returning users
  • Unified paid conversion and LTV definitions, turning activity into sustainable revenue

Six stages

02

E-COMMERCE GROWTH

From market entry to a global business

“The site loads and the budget gets spent, but growth does not move.”

E-commerce growth is a commercial chain: decide where to go and how to localise, build the place where the sale happens, then acquire, convert and drive repeat purchase, and finally run several markets at once. The order of the seven stages is itself the recommended sequence.

This path covers

  • Market, category and competitor analysis: decide where to go and which price band to lead with
  • Localised expression and DTC site / conversion infrastructure builds that catch the traffic
  • Combining multi-channel media and the influencer ecosystem to turn demand into traffic at controlled cost
  • Conversion diagnosis and a repeat purchase system: turning one-off sales into long-term relationships

Seven stages

AI + EXPERT

AI and experts do not do the same job. One narrows possibilities into candidates; the other decides which candidate is worth doing.

Intelligence · Automation · Optimization

AI

Converging data into candidates: which keywords are worth bidding on, which creative is losing volume, which market has the lower marginal cost. It improves speed and coverage - it does not decide your direction for you.

Strategy · Judgment · Creativity · Execution

EXPERT

Judging which candidate is worth doing, how to split the budget, when to stop. That determines direction, and it is the part we are accountable for.
